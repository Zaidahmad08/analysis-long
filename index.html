This is a behavioral analysis of conversation data, not a psychological diagnosis or an objective measurement of love. Numbers don't know how you feel. They can still show patterns.

🔒 This dashboard is generated from private conversation data. It runs locally in your browser and is intended for personal reflection. No network requests, no trackers.

Honest scope

What still needs a human read

Keyword analysis can't confirm these, so they are not stated as findings: who started or escalated specific arguments, whether apologies were sincere, sarcasm vs hurt, control or manipulation, "who loves whom more", personality or attachment labels, and what any reel was meant to say. Reading the flagged days in context would settle several of them.

Why no quote galleries or "top 20 messages"

Ranking messages by emotional weight needs reading them. A keyword ranking would mostly surface jokes and lots of 😭, so it isn't included.

Methodology

How this was built

Instagram exports may contain deleted/unsent messages, missing media, timezone limitations, attachment-only messages and other artifacts. Conclusions are patterns in the available dataset, not complete knowledge of the relationship.
Patterns matter more than isolated moments.
